# SlowWave supplemental list: AI companion apps
#
# These are conversational/companion AI services with documented patterns of
# harm to minors: emotional dependency, sexual roleplay accessible without
# verification, encouragement of self-harm, and bypass of age gates via
# self-reported DOB. None are covered by major DNS blocklists as of 2026.
#
# Sources that informed this list:
#   - Common Sense Media + Stanford Brainstorm AI companion safety report (Apr 2025)
#     "AI social companions are not safe for teens younger than 18"
#   - Garcia v. Character Technologies (FL 2024) - 14yo suicide, Daenerys bot
#   - Texas product liability suit v. Character.AI (Dec 2024) - autistic teen,
#     bot encouraged self-harm and parental violence
#   - YPA / FTC complaint v. Replika (Jan 2025)
#   - Senate inquiry letter to Character Technologies, Luka, Chai (Apr 2025)
#
# Format: bare domains, one per line. Convert to your target syntax
# (hosts/dnsmasq/RPZ/ABP) via build_blocklist.py.
#
# Last reviewed: 2026-05
# Refresh cadence: quarterly minimum (this space moves fast)
